# Editing modes

- Light polish: fix clarity, grammar, repetition, and rhythm without restructuring.
- Deep rewrite: reorganize around one clear promise or argument while preserving facts.
- Voice match: infer high-level traits from user-provided samples; never clone a living author's signature style.
- Factual copy edit: prioritize accuracy, attribution, ambiguity, terminology, and consistency over elegance.

When the request is ambiguous, default to light polish and preserve more of the original wording.
